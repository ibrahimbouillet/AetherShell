# Whole-film quality scorecard

## Acceptance rule

The owner sets the acceptance target to **100/100 for the entire final video**, stricter than the
generic rubric's baseline. The score is a transparent project checklist, not an Apple, Adobe, studio,
or industry certification. All 60 checks below must pass with evidence and all ten blocking gates must
pass. A strong opening cannot average away a weak later shot. Any failed check or blocking issue means
the export is not accepted; fix it, re-export, and repeat QC against the new exact file.

A check is `PASS` only when its evidence comes from the exact final export. Untested, unavailable,
unclear, or unjustified not-applicable checks remain `PENDING` and count as zero. The score is not
claimed until the final file has been decoded, its metadata checked, and the complete export watched
at normal speed with audio. Inspect the complete frame sequence for technical faults and review each
shot, transition, motion peak, text interval, and audio sync point at full resolution. The owner may
also judge the subjective result; a binary score cannot guarantee that judgement.

## Score formula

For each domain, `points = domain_weight × passed_checks / 5`. The domain weights are A 12, B 12,
C 10, D 13, E 8, F 10, G 9, H 8, I 5, J 5, K 5, and L 3, totaling 100. Acceptance requires
all domains at full weight, all 60 checks `PASS`, and all gates `PASS`. There is no rounding up,
averaging across time, or provisional publication claim.
