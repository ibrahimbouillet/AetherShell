# Creative direction — “A desktop in motion”

## Promise and tone

Show one idea: Aether gathers the apps the user chose and useful status into a composed Windows
desktop. The mood is calm, precise, alive, and confident. Motion should feel authored and continuous,
with a deliberate reveal and enough stillness to read the real interface. After Effects is a quality
reference for timing and finish, not a required application or runtime dependency.

## Picture rules

- Open on the owner's approved arrangement: scenic wallpaper, inset status bar, centered Dock, and
  genuine pinned logos. Keep the desktop itself visible and quiet. Two takes with a black wallpaper
  were rejected. The owner confirmed a cached wallpaper restoration; verify a fresh opening frame
  before filming the sequence again.
- Use actual Aether UI and live capture pixels. No invented state, fabricated statistic, replacement
  logo, or altered product screenshot.
- Keep the interface geometry crisp. Use source-native resolution; do not call an upscaled raster icon
  “4K detail.” Preserve the real window aspect and avoid camera moves that bend interface text.
- Derive color from the real product and captured scene. Correct exposure/white balance before any
  restrained creative grade. No persistent glow, fake reflections, heavy grain, or look that changes
  the appearance of status colors.
- Keep one attention focus per shot. The pointer, Dock item, and camera must support the same action.
- Any explanatory text must be short, readable at quarter size, and added as an explicit overlay layer;
  no overlay may imply it belongs to the application.

## Motion bible

- Sample choreography from elapsed time, including subframe times. The default smootherstep profile
  eases position, velocity, and acceleration to rest at each planned transition.
- Use continuous curved cursor travel, a measured Dock approach, horizontal icon tracking, a short
  focus hold, and a reveal only after the real target window is observed.
- Give major motion anticipation, a clear path, a readable peak, and a stable landing. Do not repeat
  an identical bounce or transition as decoration.
- Build blur only by blending real frames sampled over the shutter interval. Keep stationary UI crisp;
  inspect text, icon edges, and occlusions at final output size. Eight subframe camera evaluations and
  frame-ID caching cannot replace missing source updates or conceal acquisition gaps.
- Respect reduced-motion and accessibility constraints in any interactive preview. No rapid flashes,
  peripheral drift, or effect that obscures a control.

## Light, color, sound

- Treat the captured desktop as the real environment. No artificial sunlight, shadow, reflection, or
  highlight may contradict the scene or tint important status text.
- Work in a documented SDR BT.709 delivery path unless a measured, fully validated alternative is
  selected. Check color with scopes or equivalent numeric inspection; visually compare consecutive
  shots on the final export.
- `Soundtrack.*` now supplies original procedural stereo: a restrained harmonic bed, motion sweep,
  focus cue and fades, encoded as 48-kHz AAC. This selects the implementation's sound palette, not an
  accepted mix. Verify action synchronization, loudness, true peak, dynamics and low-level listening
  comfort on the exact export. Listen through more than one playback path where available. No private
  application audio or third-party music is recorded.

## Style frames and approval

Style frames must come from reviewed genuine capture and actual Aether surfaces. C5FF64A3 is now a
reviewable export with complete decode, contact-sheet privacy coverage and inspected full-resolution
opening/focus/reveal/ending images. Normal-speed playback/listening and full acceptance remain open.
Select public imagery only after the applicable review and owner selection. Do not substitute a
mockup, rejected take or the owner's screenshot.

The second rejected take exposed unrelated content after a 5.95-second capture gap. Before/after-frame
guards now reject unexpected visible surfaces; allowed app contents and window-change races still
require review of every frame. Buffered acquisition has now passed tests and completed the latest
real take. Preserve deliberate holds and continuous motion; do not call resampled 60-FPS delivery
constant-60-FPS source footage. Leave whole-film acceptance pending until playback/listening and
the remaining exact-export QC pass.
